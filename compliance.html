Regulatory Framework

CMA

Capital Markets Authority

VaTViT is designed for CMA Regulatory Sandbox entry. Our system boundary design ensures we operate as a Financial Operating System — not a broker, and not a fund manager.

  • Sandbox application in preparation
  • System boundary compliance
  • Capital requirements met
  • Annual audits scheduled
CBK

Central Bank of Kenya

Full compliance with CBK anti-money laundering guidelines and forex regulations.

  • AML transaction monitoring
  • Sanctions screening (CBK watchlists)
  • 7-year document retention
  • Quarterly CBK reporting
ODPC

Data Protection Act

All personal data of Kenyan users stored in-country with full GDPR-equivalent protection.

  • Nairobi-region data residency
  • Data Processing Agreements
  • Right-to-erasure support
  • Privacy by design
