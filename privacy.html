Trust and privacy

Privacy Policy

VaTViT is under development. This policy explains the principles we follow while our platform and early-access programme are being built.

Information we collect

When you join early access or create an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, and information you choose to provide. We collect technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide access, communicate product updates, secure accounts, prevent abuse, and meet legal or regulatory obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose or a legal requirement. Passwords are hashed, access tokens are time-limited, and we work toward Kenya-first data handling and appropriate security controls.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting the VaTViT team. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.
